About dropped shows. A Trakt ZIP may include your viewing history without listing which shows you dropped. An unfinished series could still be in progress. When Sync Dropped Shows is selected, shows already listed as dropped in the ZIP are imported automatically. The manual picker appears only if that list is missing or empty. Select any shows you want to mark as dropped by name.
